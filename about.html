About Us

Where architecture meets the written word

Blenora Built Vision is an independent journal for people who want to understand how buildings work — and why they matter. We publish in-depth articles, practical guides, and critical perspectives on the built environment.

We believe that good design writing does not celebrate buildings. It helps you understand them. Every article we publish begins with a question: what can a reader learn from this space that they can carry into their own home, their own work, or their own way of seeing?

Blenora Built Vision was founded in 2024 by a small group of architects, designers, and journalists who were tired of the glossy, surface-level coverage that dominates most design media. We wanted something slower, deeper, and more useful — writing that treats architecture not as a backdrop for lifestyle photography but as a discipline worthy of serious attention.

Our editorial focus spans residential architecture, interior design, space planning, materials, lighting, furniture, small-space living, sustainability, and modern homes. We cover built work, unbuilt ideas, historical precedents, and emerging practices. We are as interested in the placement of a door handle as we are in the orientation of a building on its site — because both are design decisions, and both shape how a space feels.

“The best design writing is accessible without being simplistic, rigorous without being academic, and beautiful without being precious. That is the standard we hold ourselves to.” — Blenora Built Vision Editorial Charter

What We Publish

We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical essays. Some pieces are close readings of a single project. Others are thematic explorations of an idea — the return of the courtyard, the case for fewer and better things, the architecture of light. We aim for a rhythm that is varied but coherent: always considered, never rushed.

We do not publish sponsored content disguised as editorial. We do not accept payment for coverage. When we partner with brands or organisations for advertising, it is clearly labelled and kept separate from our editorial stream. Our readers trust us because we tell them the truth as we see it.

Independence

We are reader-supported and editorially independent. Our only obligation is to our readers, not to advertisers, PR agencies, or the subjects of our coverage.

Depth

We take the time to understand a subject before we write about it. Our articles are researched, edited, and fact-checked. We would rather publish less and publish better.

Clarity

Good design writing should be clear without being simplistic. We explain complex ideas in plain language without sacrificing nuance or precision.

Usefulness

Every article should leave the reader with something they can use — a new way of seeing, a practical strategy, a deeper understanding of how buildings work.

Who We Are

Our contributors are architects, interior designers, journalists, and researchers based in Europe, Asia, North America, and Australia. We work with a small core editorial team and a wider network of specialist writers.

Elena Marchetti

Editor-in-Chief

Architect and writer based in Milan. Formerly a project lead at a residential practice, Elena now leads Blenora's editorial direction and writes on spatial poetics, light, and the architecture of everyday life.

Marcus Chen

Senior Editor

Interior designer and critic based in Vancouver. Marcus writes on materials, craft, and the quiet details that make interiors feel considered. He is particularly interested in the intersection of design and restraint.

Priya Sharma

Materials & Sustainability Editor

Building scientist and writer based in London. Priya covers material innovation, passive design, and the environmental realities of construction. She holds a PhD in sustainable building technology.

Architect and educator based in Lagos. David writes on small-space living, built-in furniture, and the design lessons of vernacular architecture. He teaches at the University of Lagos.

Our Editorial Charter

  1. We publish only work that we believe is accurate, useful, and honestly presented. We do not exaggerate, sensationalise, or mislead.
  2. We disclose all potential conflicts of interest. If a writer has a professional relationship with a subject, we say so.
  3. We do not accept payment for editorial coverage. Sponsored content, when it appears, is clearly labelled and kept separate from our editorial stream.
  4. We correct errors promptly and transparently. When we get something wrong, we say so and explain what we got wrong.
  5. We respect the privacy and intellectual property of the architects, designers, and photographers whose work we cover.
  6. We aim to write in clear, jargon-free language that is accessible to a general reader without being simplistic or condescending.

Support Independent Design Writing

Blenora Built Vision is reader-supported. We do not have a paywall, and we do not intend to build one. If you value the work we do, the best way to support us is to subscribe to our newsletter, share our articles with people who might find them useful, and tell us what you think.

We welcome correspondence from readers, designers, and architects. For editorial enquiries, story pitches, or feedback, write to us at editorial@blenorabuiltvision.com.
