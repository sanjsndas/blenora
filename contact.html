About Blenora Built Vision

Blenora Built Vision is an independent editorial publication devoted to interior design and architecture. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical perspectives on the built environment — not as a catalogue of pretty pictures, but as a resource for people who want to understand how buildings work and why they matter.

Our writers are architects, designers, and journalists who believe that good design writing should be accessible without being simplistic, rigorous without being academic, and beautiful without being precious. We are reader-supported and editorially independent.
