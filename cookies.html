Cookies Policy

This policy explains how Blenora Built Vision uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 15 January 2026

Blenora Built Vision ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at blenorabuiltvision.com (the "Site"). This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and the choices you have regarding their use.

By continuing to browse or use the Site, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy. If you do not agree, you should adjust your browser settings or refrain from using the Site.

In short: We use a small number of cookies to keep the Site working and to understand which articles our readers find useful. We do not use cookies to serve you targeted advertising, and we do not sell your data.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (they remain on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (they are deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by the website you are visiting) or "third-party" (set by a different domain).

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage. This policy covers all such technologies.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

Category Purpose Examples
Essential Required for the Site to function properly. These cannot be disabled. Cookie consent preference, security tokens
Analytics Help us understand how visitors interact with the Site by collecting anonymised usage data. Page views, time on page, referral source
Functional Remember your preferences and improve your experience. Language preference, search history (if applicable)

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies to serve you targeted ads, and we do not share cookie data with advertising networks.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We currently use:

  • Analytics provider: We use a privacy-respecting analytics service to understand how readers use the Site. The data collected is anonymised and aggregated. You can learn more in our Privacy Policy.
  • Newsletter platform: If you subscribe to our newsletter, our email service provider may set cookies when you interact with our sign-up form or emails.

We do not control third-party cookies. You should review the respective privacy policies of these providers for more information.

5. Your Cookie Choices

5.1 Browser settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Please note that if you block or delete cookies, some parts of the Site may not function properly. For example, we may not be able to remember your cookie preferences.

5.2 Browser-specific instructions

For more information on how to manage cookies in your browser, please refer to:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

5.3 Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Because there is no industry consensus on how DNT should be interpreted, our Site does not currently respond to DNT signals. We do, however, minimise data collection and anonymise analytics data by default.

6. Changes to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
